**D-173 — Admin plans CRUD and subscription actions** · Accepted (owner chose 1A 2A 3A 4A and the split on 2026-11-14; MVP-151) · Amends D-122

MVP-151 left open how plans are edited, what each subscription action does to the rows, which day a period ends on and
how fast an office sees the change. → **Plans** (`src/modules/platform-plans`, platform realm as D-159, raw client with a
reason): `GET /admin/plans` (every plan, by market and sort order, with `subscriptionCount`), `POST` (code
`^[A-Z][A-Z0-9_]{1,63}$`, taken → 409 RES-002), `PATCH /admin/plans/:id` (any field **but the code** — signup and the
seeds find plans by code), `DELETE` (204; only a plan **no subscription row ever used**, else **422 BIZ-022 (new)** —
history keeps its plan; deactivate with `isActive: false` instead). Prices stay **one plan row per market** (owner 4A,
D-005): `priceMonthly` (Decimal, two places) + `currency` + `market`, no price table. `features` is a record of up to 20
boolean flags: the `PLAN_FEATURES` gate the app (D-122 defaults for a missing one); other flags, such as the seeded
`customPricing`, are kept as given. **Subscription actions:** `POST /admin/offices/:id/subscription` with one of
`{action: CHANGE_PLAN, planId}` · `{action: EXTEND_PERIOD, until}` · `{action: SET_TRIAL, until}` (+ optional `reason`,
kept in the audit row), answered with the office detail. They act on the office's **current** subscription (the live
TRIALING/ACTIVE one, else the newest — D-122) inside one transaction that locks the office row: **change plan** updates
the row in place (an inactive or unknown plan → 400 `planId` `validation.planInactive`; the same plan changes and audits
nothing); **extend** sets ACTIVE until `until` — a trial turning paid starts its period now, a paid or PAST_DUE one keeps
its start, a CANCELLED/EXPIRED row is followed by a new ACTIVE row on the same plan; **trial** extends a TRIALING row's
`trialEndsAt`, starts a new TRIALING row after an ended one, and is refused on a paid subscription (**422 BIZ-023, new**:
extend it instead). `until` is the **last day included, in the office's time zone** (ends at the next local midnight,
`zonedDateTime`); a day already past → 400 `until` `validation.notPast`. Shortening is allowed (corrections), only not
into the past. Plan history lives in the platform audit log (`SUBSCRIPTION_PLAN_CHANGED`, `SUBSCRIPTION_EXTENDED`,
`SUBSCRIPTION_TRIAL_SET`, `PLAN_CREATED|UPDATED|DELETED`, old and new values), not in extra rows. **Payments never change
the subscription** (owner 3A): recording one (its own story, with the numbered receipt of owner 1A/2A) and extending are
two actions. **Entitlements at once (amends D-122's 30 s):** an action drops that office's cached entitlements, a plan
edit drops every office's (`EntitlementsService.invalidateAll`); the admin routes run in the API process, so the change
applies on the next request there and within 30 s in the worker (the per-process cache stays until a second API
instance runs). **Not done:** cancelling a subscription or marking it EXPIRED/PAST_DUE by hand, proration, a per-plan
price history, the admin's own plan list for signup ordering beyond `sortOrder`. Why: the founder sells by hand from day
one; actions are few, reversible and audited, and the access rules stay in one place (D-122).

Review additions (same PR): **`until` at most five years ahead** (400 `validation.tooFar`), checked before the date is
converted, so a typo such as 9999-12-31 can neither grant access for good nor fail as a 500. **A lapsed row** (still
TRIALING/ACTIVE in the table, its end passed — nothing writes EXPIRED) is ended for the actions: extend restarts its
period now, a trial turns it into a TRIALING row in place (the partial unique index forbids a second live row).
**Changing the plan of an ended row** is allowed and rewrites that row's plan, so the next extend uses it; the audit row
keeps the old plan. **A plan save that changes nothing** writes no audit row and drops no cache, and the admin panel
sends only the fields that changed (two admins editing different fields do not overwrite each other). A delete racing a
new subscription answers **BIZ-022** (its foreign-key failure), not a 500. **Feature defaults are shared**
(`PLAN_FEATURE_DEFAULTS` in shared-contracts): the plan form shows a flag the plan leaves out at its default, so saving
a seeded plan never switches AI or the portal off by accident.
