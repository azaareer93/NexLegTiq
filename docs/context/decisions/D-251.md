**D-251 — Office deletion: 30-day read-only grace, then one per-tenant purge that keeps issued invoices and an anonymized audit** · Accepted (MVP-152, 2026-11-27) · Builds on D-079, D-122, D-136, D-184
The DPA and FAQ promise that a leaving office's data is deleted after 30 days, but nothing deleted an office, and the
ticket left open what survives the purge. → The owner's answers (Jira, 2026-11-27) applied as follows.
**Request:** `POST /offices/me/delete-request` (`manage:office`, 5/min) re-checks the caller's password, plus a TOTP code
when the user has MFA on (`mfaEnabled`; secret context `users.mfa_secret:<userId>`, the platform convention — office MFA
enrolment does not exist yet). A wrong password, or a missing or wrong code, → 400 VAL-001 on that field (as `users/me/password`;
never a 401, which the apps treat as a lost session), already scheduled → 409 RES-003. It sets `offices.deletion_requested_at` and
`deletion_scheduled_for = now + 30 days` (fixed), writes an office audit row (`Office`, `SECURITY`, `{deletion: "REQUESTED"}`)
and emails **every active office manager** (`office-deletion` template: requested / cancelled / rescheduled).
`GET /offices/me/delete-request` shows the state; `POST /offices/me/delete-request/cancel` (any office manager, no re-auth:
undoing is safe) clears both columns, audited and emailed the same way.
**Read-only grace:** the subscription guard (D-122) refuses writes with **403 AUTH-105** while a deletion is scheduled; the
routes already `@AllowWhenReadOnly()` (export, own profile/password, notifications, downloads) stay open, and so does the
cancel. `GET /offices/me/subscription` carries `deletionScheduledFor` (every user), so the app shows a banner and its
read-only state.
**Platform:** `POST /admin/offices/:id/deletion/cancel` (reason required) at any time, and
`POST /admin/offices/:id/deletion/reschedule` `{scheduledFor, reason}` only to an **earlier** time not in the past — the
office's written request, its reference in the reason; both in the platform audit log and emailed to the office managers.
Never later: the office can always cancel itself.
**Purge** (`purge-offices`, daily 02:30 UTC on the `report` queue): for each office past `deletion_scheduled_for` and not
purged: (1) every storage object under `{officeId}/` is deleted (idempotent; a failure leaves the office for the next run);
(2) one transaction: issued invoices (with a number) are copied to `retained_invoices` as issued — office name, tax id,
registration number, client name, dates, lines, totals, status — with `retain_until = now + RETAINED_RECORDS_YEARS` (env,
default **7**, pending legal confirmation for Palestine and Jordan); then `nlq_purge_office(office_id)`, a `SECURITY DEFINER`
function (owner: migrator; refuses an office not past its date) that anonymizes the office's audit rows (user and client-user
ids, old/new values, IP, user agent and request id set to null; entity type/id, action and time kept), deletes every row with
that `office_id` in every table except `offices`, `office_settings`, `audit_logs`, `subscriptions`, `platform_payments` and
`retained_invoices` (table list read from the catalogue, so a new tenant table is purged without a change here; foreign-key
order found by retrying, a cycle aborts the purge), then deletes the office's former portal users who have no access left
anywhere; finally the office row loses its phone, email, address and website, `is_active = false`, `purged_at = now`.
Search data (tsvector columns, document texts) goes with the rows; AI cache entries expire on their TTL. A purge audit row is
written. The same job deletes `retained_invoices` past `retain_until`.
Why: one function that cannot touch an office before its date keeps the irreversible step narrow; reading tables from the
catalogue means "zero tenant rows" stays true as the schema grows; the kept set is exactly what the owner said is our own or
legally required record.
Review additions (same PR): **order is lock, storage, rows.** The purge locks the office row and re-checks it is still due
before it deletes any object, so a cancel during the run waits and then gets 409 instead of leaving a live office without its
files; after the commit a second best-effort sweep removes files a running export or PDF job wrote late. Kept platform
payments lose their stored receipt PDF reference (it lived under the office prefix); the receipt re-renders on demand. A
former portal user still referenced by another office's rows is anonymized (email, name, password) instead of deleted.
**Accepted risk:** the function enforces the scheduled date only. The app role writes that date and already has DML on every
office row, so a compromised app role could anonymize an office's audit log by setting the date to now; a trigger refusing
past dates would not stop `now() + 1 s`. A stronger barrier needs a separate setter role, left for the owner to decide.
Owner decisions (2026-11-28, PR review): **S1 — per-office pseudonym.** Before the ids are nulled, each audit row gets
`audit_logs.actor_ref`: staff "User 1", "User 2"…, portal clients "Client 1"…, one per distinct actor, numbered by first
appearance within the office, so who did what stays provable without naming anyone. Platform admin ids stay (our staff).
**S2 — one clock.** `nlq_purge_office(office_id, retain_until)` sets `offices.records_retained_until` to the same date the
retained invoices get (purge time + `RETAINED_RECORDS_YEARS`), and refuses less than one year; `nlq_purge_audit_logs` keeps
a purged office's rows, whatever their age, until that date and then deletes them all; a live office keeps the D-136 rule.
`office_settings` is no longer changed by the purge. **S3 — no hanging request.** The office's request and cancel and the
platform's cancel and reschedule set `lock_timeout = 3s` in their transaction; a wait on the office row a running purge
holds fails with PostgreSQL 55P03, mapped to **409 RES-003** (`prisma-error.ts`, api-conventions.md). **Known limits:** a TOTP
code can be replayed within its ±30 s window (no last-used step for office users until MFA enrolment exists); the read-only
flag is cached per process for up to 30 s (D-122 entitlements cache), so another API instance may accept writes for that
long after a request or cancel. **Deliberate exception:** kept `platform_payments` rows still hold the receipt snapshot's
`payerAddress` and `payerEmail` after the purge, against "remove the office's contact details", because a receipt must match
what was issued.
