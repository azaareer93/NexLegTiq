**D-175 — Demo office seed: raw rows, written once, reset by office** · Accepted (MVP-118, 2026-11-14) · Builds on D-007, D-037

MVP-118 left open how demo data is built, how "idempotent" and "resettable" relate, who may run it and how the demo users
sign in. → **One demo office** (`DEMO_OFFICE_ID`, `prisma/seed-demo.ts`): the office (with `office@demo.nexlegtiq.test`
and 16 % tax), **one user per role** (`<key>@demo.nexlegtiq.test`), an ACTIVE Professional subscription paid a year
ahead, and its content (`prisma/demo-content.ts`): 30 clients (20 people in Arabic and English, 10 companies/NGO with a
primary contact), 60 files over all 12 types (numbered with the office's own format, sequences left where the demo
stopped, team rows, `FILE_OPENED` events, a few closed/suspended/confidential), a past (adjourned, with minutes) and a
future hearing for every open litigation and criminal file (one slot per day, never double-booked), two tasks per live
file, three time entries per hourly file, eight invoices covering DRAFT/SENT/OVERDUE/PAID with gap-free `INV-` numbers,
six sample PDFs (one-page placeholders uploaded to storage) whose stored OCR text is Arabic, English or mixed, and one
portal client (`client@demo.nexlegtiq.test`). **Rows are written directly** with the seed client (not through the
services, which need a request context): each one satisfies the same constraints the services keep (composite FKs,
CHECKs, triggers make the default folders and search vectors). **Fictional by construction (D-007):** names carry
"(تجريبي)/(Demo)" or are invented placeholders, every address is on the reserved `.test` domain. **Idempotent =
written once:** the office, users and subscription are upserted on every run; the content is created only while the office
has no clients, so a re-run never duplicates or overwrites a demo someone is presenting. **Reset**
(`nx run backend-api:demo-reset` = `seed-demo.ts --reset`) deletes every tenant row of the demo office only (children
first, keeping office, settings, users and subscription), the portal user and the office's storage prefix, then seeds
again; deleting audit rows needs the owner role (D-060), so run it with the migration database URL. **Who may run it:**
`NODE_ENV=development|test`, or `SEED_DEMO=true` on a dedicated demo deployment — never production by default.
**Sign-in:** `DEMO_PASSWORD` (hashed with the app's Argon2id) for every demo user; unset = no one can sign in (a
placeholder hash). **Tests:** the integration suite resets and seeds twice on the CI database and checks the counts,
types, past/future hearings, invoice states, storage documents, `.test` addresses and the file-number counters.
Review additions (same PR): the content is written in **one transaction** (a failure leaves no half-built demo); **every run puts the demo accounts back** (role, active, verified, `DEMO_PASSWORD` — unset writes the placeholder) and refuses an email that belongs to another office; the **portal client signs in** with the same password; **reset also removes users added during a demo** (any user of the office outside the seed's list), keeps the seed users' terms acceptances, refuses a missing Prisma delegate and explains the owner-role requirement; outside development/test the seed refuses a database that has any other office (a demo deployment has its own database). The content follows the services' invariants more closely: invoice numbers by the issue year and in issue order, only OVERDUE past its due date, entries before their invoice and after their file opened, closing dates in the past, paralegal tasks only on the paralegal's files, the past hearing points at the next, and timeline events for hearings, completed tasks, sent/paid invoices and closed files. Phone numbers look Palestinian but nothing sends SMS or WhatsApp yet. **Not done:** demo AI summaries and witness statements, real scanned images (the PDFs are text placeholders; the OCR text
is stored as if extracted), Cypress fixtures built on the demo office (they keep their own sign-up flow).
