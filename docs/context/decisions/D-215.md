**D-215 — Product analytics: first-party events in our own database, counted per office, no vendor** · Accepted (MVP-110, 2026-11-21) · Builds on D-181, D-183

MVP-110 asked for onboarding-funnel and D7/D30 retention metrics with a "privacy-friendly tool (e.g. PostHog EU /
self-hosted)". Any hosted vendor is a new sub-processor (DPA, privacy policy) and possibly spend; a self-hosted
PostHog is a heavy extra service for a solo founder. The ACs fit a first-party solution, so: → **No analytics vendor,
no browser script, no cookie.** Events are rows of `product_events` (`office_id`, `event` enum `ProductEventType`,
`props` JSONB, `created_at`) in our own PostgreSQL — no new sub-processor, no spend, nothing to consent to beyond
strictly necessary (the app still sets no analytics cookie or storage). **Identity:** the office id only — no user id,
name, email, case title or document content; tenant model (`TENANT_MODELS`), cascade-deleted with its office.
**Allow-list:** `ProductEventSchema` (shared-contracts) is a discriminated union of strict objects — only `step`
(an onboarding step) on ONBOARDING_STEP_COMPLETED and `secondsSinceSignup` on FIRST_CASE_CREATED; any other key is
refused (unit-tested). **Events** (UPPER_SNAKE per our enum rule; the ticket's lower-case names map 1:1):
SIGNUP_COMPLETED (+ step ACCOUNT, which signup completes) · ONBOARDING_STEP_COMPLETED{step} (each step newly in `done`)
· ONBOARDING_COMPLETED · FIRST_CASE_CREATED{secondsSinceSignup} (when the office's file count is 1) ·
HEARING_SCHEDULED · DOCUMENT_UPLOADED (new documents, not versions) · AI_SUMMARY_GENERATED (document and session
summaries, after commit) · INVITE_SENT · PORTAL_INVITE_SENT · **APP_ACTIVE** (added: once per office and UTC day, from
the auth guard — the activity retention needs; a per-process memo keeps it to one query a day). **Where `track` lives
(deviation):** the ticket put a `track(event, props)` wrapper in shared-utils; every event is emitted **server-side**
by `ProductAnalytics.track` (backend, global provider) instead — routes declare theirs with `@TrackProductEvent(…)`,
recorded by a global interceptor once the route succeeded (case, hearing, document, both invites); signup, the
wizard and the summarizers call it directly — nothing is lost to ad blockers, nothing runs in the
browser, and shared-utils may not import the contracts that hold the allow-list. The wrapper never throws (a failure
is logged). **Opt-outs:** a request with `DNT: 1` or `Sec-GPC: 1` records nothing (CLS `doNotTrack`); the office
setting `enableProductAnalytics` (default on; Settings → Defaults, AR/EN) stops all its events. Worker events (AI
summaries) know no request, so only the office setting applies to them. Turning it off keeps past rows (counts only).
**Dashboard = admin panel** `/analytics` (platform realm, `GET /admin/analytics?days=7..365`, 90 by default): cohort =
offices whose first SIGNUP_COMPLETED is in the period; offices per funnel event (with share of signups and the
product.md targets: onboarding > 70%, D7 > 40%, D30 > 20%), per onboarding step, median time to the first case, and
retention — D7 = any APP_ACTIVE on UTC days 7–13 after the signup day, D30 = days 30–36 (activity counts any
authenticated request, also one a later guard refuses), counted only for offices whose window has
ended. **For the owner:** the privacy policy should say we keep first-party, per-office product usage counts with an
office opt-out (no new sub-processor). **Not done:** retention purge of `product_events`, per-user metrics, a
weekly-cohort chart, a vendor export.
