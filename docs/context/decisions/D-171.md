**D-171 — Invoice PDFs: Chromium's own print, one stored PDF per invoice, email on send** · Accepted (MVP-92, 2026-11-14)
MVP-92 left the renderer, when a PDF is made and kept, the language and the recipient open. → **Rendering:** the worker
prints an HTML template with headless **Chromium's `--print-to-pdf`** through `execFile` (`modules/invoices/pdf/chromium.ts`,
the D-139 pattern) — no Playwright or Puppeteer dependency. The worker image adds Debian's `chromium` (amends D-103);
GitHub's runners already have Google Chrome; `PDF_CHROMIUM_PATH` (default `chromium`) points elsewhere locally. The page is
our template (`invoice-pdf.html.ts`) with **every value escaped**, no script and no external resource; the fonts are the
self-hosted IBM Plex Sans Arabic and Inter (D-088), embedded as data URIs, so no font packages or network are needed
(deviation: D-088 named Amiri for documents — Plex is already shipped and reads well on an invoice). Chromium runs with a
private profile in a temporary folder, `--no-sandbox` (the container drops the capabilities its sandbox needs) and none of
the server's secrets in its environment. `--blink-settings=scriptEnabled=false` is **not** used: it stops the print itself.
Layout: A4, `dir="rtl"` for Arabic, logical CSS, numbers, codes and money isolated (`<bdi>`), amounts by `formatMoney`
in the currency's digits (D-092), dates dd/MM/yyyy. Shown: office name, address, phone, email, tax id and registration
number; client name, address, phone, email; the file number and title; lines; subtotal, tax (only when there is tax), total;
notes. **Not shown yet:** a logo (no office asset storage, D-125) and payment instructions (no such setting — offices write
them in the invoice notes). **Storage:** one PDF per invoice at `{officeId}/invoices/{id}/{uuid}.pdf`, recorded in new columns
`pdf_storage_key`, `pdf_language`, `pdf_rendered_for` (migration `invoice_pdf`); the PDF is **current** while
`pdf_rendered_for` equals the invoice's `updatedAt` and its language matches. The worker records it **without changing
`updatedAt`**, conditionally on `updatedAt` being unchanged — an invoice edited, paid or voided during the print fails the
attempt (the retry prints the new state) — and deletes the previous object. **API:** `GET /invoices/:id/pdf?language=AR|EN`
(same rights and visibility as `GET /invoices/:id`, D-162; default the office's language, BILINGUAL → Arabic) → **200**
`{status: READY, downloadUrl, expiresIn: 300}` (presigned attachment named after the invoice number, D-085) with a
`DOWNLOAD` audit row, or **202** `{status: RENDERING}` after queuing `report/render-invoice-pdf` (job id per invoice state,
language and minute, so repeated asks queue once and a failed render can be asked for again a minute later); the app asks
again. "Regenerated on edit" is this rule: any change makes the next ask render again. **Send:** `POST /invoices/:id/send`
takes an optional `{emailClient, language}`; every send queues the PDF after the commit, and with `emailClient` the worker
emails it (template `invoice`, AR/EN, PDF attached) to the client's **primary contact person's address, else the client's
own**; with neither, the send is refused **400 VAL-001 `validation.noClientEmail`** before anything changes. The email goes
last in the job, through the mail transport directly (attachments do not belong in Redis), with the job id as Resend's
idempotency key (SMTP may deliver twice after a lost answer, as D-085). The send's audit row records `emailClient`.
**Deviation:** "optionally sets `sharedWithClient` for the portal" is left to the portal invoices story, which owns the share
flag. **Not done:** emailing again after the send (send is once), a copy to the office, per-office templates.
