**D-200 — Client portal app: phone-first screens on the portal realm; office contact details in the portal session** · Accepted (MVP-100, 2026-11-21) · Builds on D-003, D-158, D-186, D-187, D-188

MVP-100 asked for the portal UI with "office branding (name/logo) from portal `me`" and a "contact your lawyer" card, but
`me` carried only the office name and offices have no logo. → **Contract:** `PortalOfficeAccess` (in `me`, login, refresh and
accept-invite) gains `officePhone` and `officeEmail` (nullable, the office's own public `phone`/`email`), read in
`PortalAccounts.offices()`; nothing about the staff (D-186: the lawyer is shown by name only). **Brand:** the shell shows the
office's name when the account has one office, the product name when it spans several (each file and invoice then names its
office). **App** (`apps/client-portal`, realm `portal`, `portalAuthApi` + `portalApi` in shared-api-client, keys in the new
`portal` i18n namespace, Arabic by default): `/login`, `/forgot-password` (always the same answer), `/reset-password?token`,
`/accept-invite?token` (a new address sets name + password with the policy hint; an existing account — `accountExists` — is
asked only for its current password, and the invited client's name fills the contract's required `fullName`, which the server
ignores); signed in: `/` my files (status, number, next hearing with the court in the reading language, one contact card per
office), `/files/:id` with tabs in the URL (`overview` = status, next hearing, the public timeline as **portal-only labels by
kind and date** — never the office's sentences, which interpolate payloads — and the contact card with the lawyer's name;
`documents` = shared documents downloaded through the audited 5-minute link; `uploads` = send a file, one idempotency key per
pick, then its review status and rejection reason in portal wording), `/invoices` (paid/unpaid/overdue, total in its currency,
PDF: READY downloads, RENDERING says "being prepared"). A 404 file shows "not available"; tokens of emailed links leave the
address bar at once and the page sets `no-referrer`. The access token stays in memory (D-050); no idle sign-out (the portal
session is the 7/30-day refresh of D-158). `zodRule`/`parseForm` are a third copy of the office/admin helpers (`shortcut:`).
**Not done:** office logo (no column or upload yet — a branding story); the office-side share buttons for documents/invoices
(D-186/D-188 left them to this story, but they are office-app work outside its AC); the Cypress portal journey at 390×844
(needs the seeded portal realm in the e2e stack — Vitest journeys in `ar` and `en` with axe cover the screens); automatic
re-polling of a RENDERING invoice PDF (`shortcut:`); `/legal/*` pages on the portal origin (the office app links the same paths).
Why: the smallest portal that lets a client on a phone see where the case stands, get what was shared and send what was asked,
without any internal term or staff detail crossing the boundary.
