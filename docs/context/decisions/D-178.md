**D-178 — Invoicing UI: the server drafts, the app shows; an Invoices menu item for invoice viewers** · Accepted (MVP-148, 2026-11-14) · Builds on D-162, D-171, D-176

MVP-148 left open how the wizard previews an invoice, where the office-wide list lives and who sees what. → **Wizard**
(Billing tab → "Generate invoice", `generate:invoice`, not on an archived file): client (the file's, primary first),
work period (default: the first of this month → today), "add the office's tax", extra items (description, quantity,
unit price) and notes → **"Prepare draft" creates the DRAFT on the server** (`POST /invoices`); the preview shows the
draft's own lines and totals. The app **never sums money** — the server's rounding per currency (D-162) is the only
arithmetic. From the preview: send (optionally emailing the PDF to the client, D-171), delete the draft, or keep it
(it then shows in the lists with Send). **Billing tab** (for `view:all|assigned:invoices`, as the tab itself, D-096): the
**retainer card** (balance, the five latest movements, "Record deposit" for `mark:invoice:paid`, D-176) and the file's
invoices. **`/invoices`** — a new menu item for `view:assigned:invoices` (OM, SL, L, P, A) between Documents and
Reports: the invoices the user may see, filters in the URL (status, client — listed for `manage:clients` holders —,
issued from/to), 20 per page. **Overdue** shows as an error tag with a warning icon and the word "Overdue" (never colour
alone); drafts show "Draft" instead of a number; a consolidated invoice shows "Several files". **Row actions by state
and right:** Send (DRAFT, `generate:invoice`), Mark paid (SENT/OVERDUE, `mark:invoice:paid`; date, method — RETAINER
offered only on a single-file invoice —, reference), Void (SENT/OVERDUE, `generate:invoice`, after a confirm), PDF
(anything sent; 202 → "being prepared, try again", ready → downloaded through a link click). Payment and deposit dates are days in the **office time zone** (today and "not in the future" by the
office clock, sent as noon there), never the browser's. The client filter searches on the server (20 at a time); an
unknown status or a broken page in the URL is ignored. Every write refreshes the
invoice lists, the retainer and the billing tab's entries (drafting links entries, voiding frees them). **Not done:**
editing a draft's extra items after preparing it (delete and prepare again), a Cypress journey (component tests cover the
wizard, the list and the actions), sorting the list by column.
