**D-174 — Manual payments and numbered receipts** · Accepted (owner chose 1A 2A 3A on 2026-11-14; MVP-170) · Builds on D-005, D-162, D-171, D-173

MVP-170 left open where payments live, how receipts are numbered across offices, who the receipt names and how it
reaches the office. → **Schema** (migration `add_platform_payments`): `PlatformPayment` (office, `receiptNumber` unique,
`amount Decimal(14,3)` rounded to its currency's digits as D-162, `currency`, `method` `PlatformPaymentMethod` CASH |
BANK_TRANSFER | OTHER, `reference?`, `paidAt` date, `recordedById` → PlatformAdmin, `voidedAt/voidedById/voidReason`, the
stored receipt `pdfStorageKey/pdfLanguage/pdfRenderedFor` as invoices) — a **tenant model** (it has `officeId`; only the
platform realm reaches it, through the raw client) — and `ReceiptNumberSequence` (one row per year, global). CHECKs:
amount > 0, number `^RCT-\d{4}-\d{4,}$`, void time and reason together, PDF key under the office; the app role may not
DELETE or TRUNCATE payments. **Numbering (owner 2A):** `RCT-{YEAR}-{SEQ:4}`, **one sequence for the whole platform** (the
issuer is our company, not the office), year of the record date in **Asia/Hebron** (the launch market), gap-free with
the D-095 counter row and lock; a mistaken payment is **voided**, keeps its number, and is never deleted. **Receipt
(owner 1A):** a plain "payment receipt — not a tax invoice", no VAT line; it names the issuer from new settings
`RECEIPT_ISSUER_NAME` (default `NexLegTiq`), `RECEIPT_ISSUER_ADDRESS`, `RECEIPT_ISSUER_TAX_ID` (unset lines are left off —
set them before the first real receipt), the office (name, address, email), date, amount, method and reference; a voided
receipt is stamped "Void". Printed like invoices (Chromium, D-171) by `report/render-payment-receipt`, run **in the paying
office's context** (TenantRunner), so it is stored at `{officeId}/receipts/{id}/…` and the producer stamps the office;
the `report` processor now accepts a job matching either job schema and parses each by job name (closing D-171's known
limit). Language: the office's (`BILINGUAL` → Arabic). **Payments never change the subscription (owner 3A).** **API**
(platform realm, D-145): `GET /admin/offices/:id/payments` (newest paid first), `POST` the same path `{amount, currency,
method, reference?, paidAt, emailOffice = true}` → 201 and queues the receipt (emailed to the **office's own address**
when `emailOffice`; refused 400 `emailOffice` `validation.noOfficeEmail` when it has none); more decimals than the
currency has → 400 `amount`; a paid day after today in the office's zone → 400 `paidAt` `validation.futureDate`. `POST
/admin/payments/:id/void {reason}` (3–500 characters) → 200, a second void **422 BIZ-024 (new)**. `GET
/admin/payments/:id/receipt?language=` → 200 `{READY, downloadUrl, expiresIn: 300}` or 202 `{RENDERING}` (queued once per
state, language and minute), as D-171. Platform audit rows `PAYMENT_RECORDED`, `PAYMENT_VOIDED`,
`PAYMENT_RECEIPT_DOWNLOADED`. **Admin panel:** a Payments card on the office page — table, Record payment, Void with a
reason, Receipt (opens the link, or says it is being prepared). Review additions (same PR): **the payer is frozen** — the office's name, address and email at recording are stored on the payment (`payer_*`, migration `platform_payment_payer`) and every re-print shows them, so a number once issued never names a different payer; the email still goes to the office's current address. **The receipt year is the year the payment is recorded** (Hebron time), not the paid day: a payment of 31 December recorded on 2 January takes the new year's number. A queue outage after the commit is logged, not answered with 500 (a retry would record a duplicate number): the receipt can still be rendered from the receipt route, but the email is then not sent. A report job whose payload does not match **its own** job's schema fails at once (UnrecoverableError, D-084). Amounts compare significant decimals (`10.500` ILS is fine); a paid day before 2020 is refused; the list returns the newest 100; a CHECK guards the currency code. The admin panel follows the receipt link with an anchor click (no popup blocker). **Not done:** re-sending the email, a receipt for a
suspended office while it is suspended (office jobs skip suspended offices, D-082 — the link works again on reactivation),
refunds, payment totals or reports, online payments (Phase 4, D-005). Why: the founder sells by hand from day one; each
payment is recorded once with a receipt the office can keep, and corrections leave a trail.
