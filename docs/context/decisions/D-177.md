**D-177 — Task reminders: one daily pass at 08:00 office time, worked out from the tasks as they are** · Accepted (MVP-79, 2026-11-14) · Builds on D-117, D-125, D-146, D-149

MVP-79 asked for a delayed job per task (moved on a due-date change, removed on completion or deletion) and a daily
digest. → **Deviation: no per-task jobs.** Every hour a platform job (`reminder/task-reminders-tick`, minute 5 UTC) queues
`task-daily` for each active office where it is then 08:xx — job id `task-daily-{local date}`, so once per office and
day. That job reads the office's open tasks **as they are at that moment**: a due date moved, a task completed or
deleted, or an assignee changed needs no job to move or cancel, and nothing is lost when Redis is (D-117 needed a nightly
reconciliation for exactly that). Same result as the AC at one fixed hour; reminders arrive at 08:00 rather than at the
moment the window opens. **Per assignee (active users only):** one in-app `TASK_DUE` notification for each task due in
one of `OfficeSettings.taskReminderDays` days (default `[1]`; 0 is the due day, which the digest covers); one in-app digest
(`shell.notifications.taskDigest`: counts of overdue and due-today tasks) when there is any; and, unless the user turned
**task emails** off, one `task-digest` email (AR/EN by the user's language) listing all of it grouped by file, each task
marked overdue / due today / due on. Nothing is sent when a user has nothing. Ids per task and due date, per user and day,
make a re-run of the day a no-op. **Preferences:** `TASKS` joins `EMAIL_CATEGORIES` (the profile shows its email switch);
in-app follows the TASKS switch as before (D-146). The `reminder` processor now takes either job's payload and parses each
by job name (an unknown or mismatched payload fails at once, D-084). **Not done:** per-user reminder times, reminders for
unassigned tasks, a digest for tasks of files the user follows but is not assigned to.
