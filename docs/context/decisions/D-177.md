**D-177 — Task reminders: one daily pass at 08:00 office time, worked out from the tasks as they are** · Accepted (MVP-79, 2026-11-14) · Builds on D-117, D-125, D-146, D-149

MVP-79 asked for a delayed job per task (moved on a due-date change, removed on completion or deletion) and a daily
digest. → **Deviation: no per-task jobs.** Every hour a platform job (`reminder/task-reminders-tick`, minute 5 UTC) queues
`task-daily` for each active office where it is then 08:xx — job id `task-daily-{local date}`, so once per office and
day. That job reads the office's open tasks **as they are at that moment**: a due date moved, a task completed or
deleted, or an assignee changed needs no job to move or cancel, and there are no per-task jobs to lose (D-117 needed a nightly
reconciliation for those). Same result as the AC at one fixed hour; reminders arrive at 08:00 rather than at the
moment the window opens. **Per assignee (active users only):** one in-app `TASK_DUE` notification for each task due in
one of `OfficeSettings.taskReminderDays` days (default `[1]`; 0 is the due day, which the digest covers); one in-app digest
(`shell.notifications.taskDigest`: counts of overdue and due-today tasks) when there is any; and, unless the user turned
**task emails** off, one `task-digest` email (AR/EN by the user's language) listing all of it grouped by file, each task
marked overdue / due today / due on. Nothing is sent when a user has nothing; a user with only due-soon tasks gets their reminders and the email listing them, but no digest notification (there is nothing overdue or due today). Ids per task and due date, per user and day,
make a re-run of the day a no-op. **Preferences:** `TASKS` joins `EMAIL_CATEGORIES` (the profile shows its email switch);
in-app follows the TASKS switch as before (D-146). The `reminder` processor now takes either job's payload and parses each
by job name (an unknown or mismatched payload fails at once, D-084). Review additions (same PR): **only tasks the assignee may still see count** — no file, or a live OPEN/SUSPENDED file in their case scope (the rule of `colleagueMay`); a deleted, closed or archived file, or one they lost access to, is neither shown nor mailed (an archived task cannot be completed, so it would nag forever). Reminder ids carry the **run day** (two reminder days of one task are two reminders). One office's failure does not stop the tick for the others; one user's failure does not stop the others (the job then retries, the ids make it a no-op for those done) and the attempt stops on its timeout signal. The email lists at most 20 files × 50 tasks (its count stays the total); the in-app reminder shows the due date as stored (ISO) until the notification list formats date params. **Known limit:** the pass runs in the 08:xx hour only — if the worker or Redis is down for that whole hour, that day's reminders are skipped (the next day's pass still lists everything overdue). The email has no counts in its subject and the in-app digest puts the numbers after their labels, so Arabic needs no plural forms. **Not done:** a catch-up pass, per-user reminder times, reminders for
unassigned tasks, a digest for tasks of files the user follows but is not assigned to.
