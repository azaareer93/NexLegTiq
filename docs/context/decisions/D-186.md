**D-186 — Portal data: one portal account reads each office in its own tenant context, in portal-only shapes; sharing is an explicit, audited toggle** · Accepted (MVP-98, 2026-11-16) · Builds on D-003, D-158, D-126

MVP-98 left open how a portal account that spans offices (D-158) reaches tenant data, what each route shows and how sharing
is recorded. → **Scope:** the account's grants come from `PortalAccounts.offices` (live grant, active office and client, the
portal in the plan); each office is read through `TenantRunner` in its own tenant context, filtered to **files linked to the
grant's client** (`FileClient`, live files — a confidential file still reaches its own client). No office id comes from the
request: `GET /portal/files` lists every office's files with `officeId`/`officeName`; a `:id` route finds the office whose
client the file or document belongs to, else **404 RES-001** (another client in the same office included). **Shapes**
(`portal.contract.ts`, never the office DTOs): file = number, title, status, the responsible lawyer's **name**, the next
hearing that is not cancelled and not started (time and court name); detail adds the newest 100 timeline events of
`PORTAL_TIMELINE_EVENT_TYPES` as **kind and time only** — payloads hold names, amounts and actors, and a document shared
and later unshared must not leak its name; documents = shared, live and **scanned clean** (PROCESSING or READY: a
document whose extraction failed after a clean scan is left out, as is everything not yet scanned). **Download:** the
5-minute link of D-128, audited `DOWNLOAD` with `clientUserId` and no `userId`. **Sharing** (`POST documents/:id/share|unshare`,
`share:document` = OM, SL, L; file access `contribute`): under a lock on the document; a change is audited (UPDATE of
`sharedWithClient`), a share also adds DOCUMENT_SHARED to the timeline (without the name of a confidential document); setting
the current value writes nothing; a quarantined document is never shared (STO-004). **Unshare** needs only read access to the document and works in a read-only office (`@AllowWhenReadOnly`) and on an archived file: the portal still shows both, so withdrawing must never be blocked. A file two grants of one office reach (a person and their company) is listed once. A DOCUMENT_SHARED kind stays on the client's timeline after an unshare (no name, accepted). **Not done:** paging (a client has few
files; `shortcut:` at 200 files and 200 documents per file), client uploads (MVP-99; `me` is `auth/me`, D-158), portal invoices (MVP-150), the office-side share button (the portal UI story, MVP-100).
