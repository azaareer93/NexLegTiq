**D-188 — Portal invoices: an explicit, audited share flag on issued invoices; the PDF is the office's own render** · Accepted (MVP-150, 2026-11-16) · Builds on D-003, D-162, D-171, D-186

MVP-150 left open who may share an invoice, which ones a client sees and how the PDF reaches the portal. → **Flag:**
`invoices.shared_with_client` (default false). `POST /invoices/:id/share|unshare` with **`generate:invoice`** (the roles who
issue invoices; no new matrix row), under the invoice's row lock: only a SENT, OVERDUE or PAID invoice is shared (a draft or a
void one → 422 BIZ-006); unshare works in any state and in a read-only office (`@AllowWhenReadOnly`, as documents, D-186). A
change is audited (UPDATE of `sharedWithClient`); a share adds **INVOICE_SHARED** (new timeline type, not on the portal's
public timeline) to every file the invoice bills, like INVOICE_SENT. Setting the current value writes nothing. Sharing keeps
`updatedAt`, so the stored PDF stays current (D-171 ties currency to `updatedAt`).
**Portal:** `GET /portal/invoices` lists, for every office the account may enter (D-158), the shared invoices **of the grant's
client** that are SENT, OVERDUE or PAID — an invoice voided after sharing disappears — in a portal shape (number, status,
currency, dates, total in the currency's digits, paid time, the billed file). `GET /portal/invoices/:id/pdf` answers as the
office route does (200 with a 5-minute link once the PDF of the invoice as it is now exists, else 202 and a render queued,
in the office's default language), audited DOWNLOAD with `clientUserId` and no `userId`; anything else is 404 (RES-001).
**Not done:** paging (newest 200 per office, `shortcut:` as files); a share button in the office app (the invoices UI follow-up);
emailing the client on share.
